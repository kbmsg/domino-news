## The Problem: Missing Critical Security Updates

Let's face it: keeping up with security bulletins can be a pain. HCL regularly publishes security advisories for Domino, Notes, and Sametime, but if you're not actively checking their official channels, it's easy to miss them. And in our line of work, missing a critical update isn't just an inconvenience—it can be a disaster waiting to happen.

## The Solution: Subscribe to HCL's Security Bulletins

To stay ahead of potential threats, you need these bulletins delivered directly to you. Here's how to set that up:

1. **Visit HCL's Security Bulletin Page**: Navigate to HCL's official security bulletin page. This is where they post all their advisories and updates.

2. **Sign Up for Notifications**: Look for an option to subscribe or sign up for notifications. This might be a mailing list, RSS feed, or another form of alert system. (If HCL doesn't offer a direct subscription option, consider setting up a custom RSS feed or using a third-party service to monitor the page for changes.)

3. **Verify Your Subscription**: After signing up, ensure you're receiving the notifications. Check your spam or junk folders to make sure the emails aren't being filtered out.

## Why This Matters

By subscribing to these bulletins, you'll receive timely information about vulnerabilities, patches, and critical updates. This proactive approach allows you to address issues before they become problems, ensuring your Domino environment remains secure and compliant.
